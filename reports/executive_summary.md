# Executive Summary

All figures describe synthetic data generated by `data/make_dataset.py` from `data/config/synthetic.yaml`. They come from one `make pipeline` run (seed 42) and are not measurements of a real game or a real marketing budget.

## Key KPIs (synthetic data)

- **Funnel (ratios of stage totals):** Install to Onboarding 95.6%, Onboarding to D1 47.6%, D1 to D7 73.2%, D7 to Purchase 16.8%. The stages are not nested cohorts: 39.3% of D7 returners have no D1 return.
- **Retention:** Overall D1 45.5%, D7 33.3%; Organic D1 47.7%, D7 35.2%. Among players who returned on D1, 44.4% also returned on D7 (Organic 45.5%).
- **ROI/ROAS:** Organic ROAS 1.85 (ROI +85%); paid channels range 0.19-0.34 ROAS (ROI -66% to -81%). These differences come entirely from the CAC values in the config; the generator has no channel effect on purchases or revenue.

## What Is Recovered

The retention channel effects set in the config are recovered from the pipeline output within 3 standard errors, as checked by `tests/test_channel_effects.py`. This claim covers those retention effects only.

The churn model is a secondary exercise. Its results and limits are in the README.
